Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Transovo AI LLC develops and operates cloud software platforms and business communications technology. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our websites or use our services.

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, company name, and message content when you contact us. We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, and usage data to operate and improve our websites and services.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers who assist in operating our platforms, with advisors when required, or when disclosure is required by law, regulation, or legal process.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or comply with legal requirements.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information by contacting us at support@transovo.ai, subject to applicable law.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
